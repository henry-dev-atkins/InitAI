# Maintain Terminology

The reason for this is NOT to avoid words, but to avoid IDEAS. 

Prohibited Idea Types:

1) Software Complexity:
  - `canary`
  - `manifest`
  - `certificate`
  - `canonical`
  - `placeholder`
  - `proof`
  - `documented/classified/validated gap`

2) Stateful Repo
  - `legacy`
  - `version X`
  - `migrate`

2) AI Commentary
  - "That was wrong",
  - "Next time I should",
  - "Instead I should have",
