# Verbosity review

Keep the answer's first line useful.

Remove greetings, praise, process narration, generic headings, repeated conclusions, and offers that do not help the requested task.

Keep one idea per sentence where a misread would matter.

Keep evidence, conditions, uncertainty, and the next required action.

Prefer a short paragraph over a list when the items form one argument.

Prefer a table when several exact fields repeat across items.

Do not enforce a word count that removes nuance or proof.

Do not replace repository terms with synonyms.

Do not use a readability score as proof of quality.

Return either the revised text or a short list of concrete cuts, as requested.

For a final agent response, also follow the communication limits in `AGENTS.md`.
